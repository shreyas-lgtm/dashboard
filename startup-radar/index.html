University Startup Radar

The top 10 startups to watch out of each of five leading university ecosystems — MIT, Harvard, Stanford, UC Berkeley, and Carnegie Mellon — ranked by momentum in the current press and accelerator cycle. Refreshed every week.

Method & sources. Each school's list is compiled weekly from its own venues (MIT delta v & The Engine, Harvard i-lab & President's Innovation Challenge, StartX, Berkeley SkyDeck & BAIR, CMU Swartz Center) plus funding coverage in TechCrunch, Bloomberg, Forbes, Fortune and Businesswire. Funding figures are as reported by the linked source; click a startup's name to open it.

Physical Intelligence appears under both Stanford and Berkeley — it was co-founded by faculty from both. Rankings are editorial, weighted toward the freshest news.
